Enterprise customers are not asking whether AI can write code. They are asking a harder question: can we prove that this exact change is ready to ship, under our policy, with a human still accountable?

That need is real. Our approved internal research found direct release-validation cases, broader AI-governance signals, and repeated demand for traceable evidence. AgentProof turns that customer ask into a reusable GitHub Copilot App workflow without exposing customer data.

A pull request starts no-secret collectors for tests, dependencies, data handling, and origin. Policy comes from the protected base, so the proposed change cannot weaken its own rules. Read-only Copilot specialists explain the same commit-bound facts. The Evidence Board Canvas turns them into one release-control surface.

Here is the Canvas opened on validated synthetic pull request number two. It binds the full head SHA, protected policy, five passes, and one unknown in one view. Missing retention evidence is not silently converted into a pass. One unresolved fact keeps the release blocked.

A reviewer selects the unresolved finding, and the Canvas drafts an exact full-SHA command. It still does not approve anything. Only an eligible human can submit that decision in GitHub. When code changes, earlier evidence and dispositions become stale. Agents can propose; they cannot approve, merge, or release.

This is a field kit, not a one-off demo: four agent profiles, two reusable skills, an Evidence Board canvas, policy and evidence schemas, GitHub Actions, setup templates, threat modeling, and measurable adoption steps. Account teams can adapt the pattern without copying customer data.

Claude Code and Copilot can both edit code and use connected tools. Our differentiation is not a model-quality claim. It is the demonstrated GitHub-native handoff across repository context, checks, artifacts, comments, reviews, rules, specialist agents, and a human decision surface.

We also tested automation permissions. Even after selecting read-only tools, the runtime exposed mutation-capable tools, so the canary failed closed and we disabled it. That is our product feedback and our design principle: agents accelerate the work; current evidence and accountable humans decide what ships.
