## Supporting limitations, not an ending slide

The prototype uses manual specialist launches and assembly. The Evidence
Board is mutable coordination; GitHub records and rules are authoritative.
The automatic-reviewer permission canary remains disabled after
`UNSAFE_TOOL_BOUNDARY`. Evidence is bounded to a repository, policy, and
commit, not a universal security, legal/compliance, or authorship assurance.

Keep these facts in the submission and the feasibility scene. Do not bury
them, and do not let an exhaustive limitation inventory displace the actual
product proof.
